How to Borrow Books

Discover how to browse our shelves, request books online, and manage your active loans.

1. Browse & Request Online

Log into your reader account and browse the Book Catalogue. Click Request book on any available title to reserve it for borrowing.

2. Library Pickup

Once library staff confirm your request, visit our physical reading room at {{ content.contact_address or 'MK Complex 2nd floor Chennai Hot Puffs upstairs, K.Pudur, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625007' }} during opening hours ({{ content.contact_hours or 'Tue–Sun: 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM & 4:30 PM – 8:30 PM' }}).

3. Borrowing Limits & Due Dates

Active members can hold up to their plan's book limit at any time. Due dates and renewal options are fully tracked inside your Member Dashboard.
